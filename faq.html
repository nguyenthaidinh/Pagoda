Frequently Asked Questions

Are files uploaded to a server?

Core processing is designed to run locally in the browser, so your files stay on your device for ordinary PDF operations.

Is PagodaPDF open source?

Yes. PagodaPDF is released under AGPL-3.0-only, with license notices retained for included third-party components.

Does it need a database?

No database is required for the current static, browser-based tool experience.
